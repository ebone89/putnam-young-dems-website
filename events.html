Saturday, May 2, 2026 · 12:00 p.m. to 2:00 p.m.

ICE OUT Cream Social

Putnam County Democratic Center
914 St. Johns Ave., Suite 2, Palatka, FL 32177

The public launch event for PCYD. Come meet your neighbors, learn about what we are building, and sign up to be part of the founding chapter. This event is open to anyone interested in civic engagement and local Democratic organizing.

What to expect: introductions, a brief overview of PCYD's goals, a chance to sign up as a founding member, and conversation with local Democrats of all ages. Refreshments provided.

Monthly Meeting

Putnam County Democratic Center
914 St. Johns Ave., Suite 2, Palatka, FL 32177

PCYD's first monthly member meeting. All members and prospective members are welcome. Expect officer updates, agenda items, and planning for upcoming outreach and events ahead of the 2026 qualifying window.

June 8 through June 12, 2026

2026 Precinct Qualifying Window

Putnam County Supervisor of Elections

The qualifying window for Democratic Precinct Committeeman and Committeewoman seats opens at noon on Monday, June 8 and closes at noon on Friday, June 12, 2026. If you are interested in running for a precinct seat, PCYD can help you understand the process and eligibility requirements.

Basic eligibility: You must reside in the precinct and must have been a registered Democrat for at least one year before qualifying begins. Confirm current form versions and filing instructions with the Putnam County Supervisor of Elections before filing.
