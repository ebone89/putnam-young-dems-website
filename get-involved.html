Get Involved

There are several ways to participate, whether you want to lead, volunteer, or simply stay connected.

Ways to Participate

Become a Founding Member

Sign the charter packet and help establish PCYD as a recognized chapter of the Florida Young Democrats. Founding members shape the organization from the ground up and are recognized in our charter documents.

Volunteer

Help with event logistics, voter registration drives, outreach, and community engagement. No prior political experience required. We will train you.

Run for a Precinct Seat

Democratic Precinct Committeeman and Committeewoman seats carry formal voting authority within the county party structure. PCYD will help eligible members understand the process and prepare to qualify.

Support as an Ally

Not in the 14 to 39 age range? You can still support PCYD as an ally by attending events, sharing our work, and connecting us with resources in the community.

Who Can Join?

Florida Young Democrats publicly describes its chapters as serving Democrats and left-leaning residents ages 14 through 39. PCYD uses that public age range for recruitment and outreach. You do not need to be a registered Democrat to attend events or volunteer, but formal membership and officer eligibility will follow charter requirements and adopted bylaws.

If you are interested but unsure whether you qualify, reach out. We would rather answer your question than lose a potential member.

What to Expect

PCYD is still in its founding phase. That means the people who get involved now will have a direct hand in shaping the organization's structure, priorities, and culture. Early participants should expect regular meetings, a collaborative atmosphere, and real responsibility.

This is not a social club. PCYD is building toward concrete goals: filling precinct seats, running voter engagement operations, and creating a pipeline of trained local Democratic leaders. If that appeals to you, you are in the right place.

Ready to Join?

The best first step is to come to our next monthly meeting or reach out directly.
